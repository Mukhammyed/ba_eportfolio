Final capstone · November 2026

A three-horizon AI plan for Kazatomprom

Deck (PDF) Report (PDF) Video Evidence

Recommendation

NAC Kazatomprom JSC should execute a three-horizon AI transformation: (1) pilot continuous SCADA downhole telemetry and machine-learning chemical dosing for 90 days on one wellfield block at the Inkai mine to remove the 48–72 hour lab assay bottleneck and reduce sulfuric acid use by a targeted 5%–6% per kg of uranium [6]; (2) adapt AI multimodal freight tracking on the Trans-Caspian International Transport Route (TITR) to cut average European delivery time by a targeted 10 days [9]; and (3) pilot one autonomous robotic drum-packing arm at the Ulba Metallurgical Plant to reduce worker radiation exposure hours by a targeted 30% [3], while keeping emergency well-block shutdowns, Subsoil Code groundwater protection and financial statement verification under human control [1] [7].

The 5%–6% acid, 10-day TITR and 30% radiation figures are proposed pilot targets benchmarked against IAEA and Cameco documentation [3] [6]. They are not verified historical results from Kazatomprom.

§1 Context

Kazatomprom faces an acid shortage [8], long lab delays [6] and a shift of about 48% of Western deliveries to the TITR [9].

§2 Stakeholders

Samruk-Kazyna owns 75% and expects dividends and domestic investment [1]. The Ministry of Energy and the Subsoil Code protect groundwater [7].

§4 Human–AI operating model

Human only: emergency shutdowns, licensing, Subsoil compliance, contracts. Human + AI: reserve modelling and inventory planning. AI with human review: dosing alerts (Lead Engineer gate above ±5%), TITR ETA variance above 24 h, ratio checks. Automated: telemetry filtering and drum filling at Ulba [3] [6] [10].

§5a Operations

A 90-day telemetry pilot replaces the 48–72 hour lab lag. Pressure above 12 bar alerts the Environmental Safety Director; drift above 3% triggers manual rounds [6].

§5b Marketing and customer

Utility customers value reliable delivery. AI predicts ETAs; the Logistics Officer confirms them. A port closure over 72 hours goes to the Commercial Director [3] [9].

§5c Workforce

Staff need human, meta and digital capabilities [4] [5], mainly the skill to verify AI outputs.
