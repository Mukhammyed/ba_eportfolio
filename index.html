My recommendation

NAC Kazatomprom JSC should execute a three-horizon AI transformation: (1) pilot continuous SCADA downhole telemetry and machine-learning chemical dosing for 90 days on one wellfield block at the Inkai mine to remove the 48–72 hour lab assay bottleneck and reduce sulfuric acid use by a targeted 5%–6% per kg of uranium [6]; (2) adapt AI multimodal freight tracking on the Trans-Caspian International Transport Route (TITR) to cut average European delivery time by a targeted 10 days [9]; and (3) pilot one autonomous robotic drum-packing arm at the Ulba Metallurgical Plant to reduce worker radiation exposure hours by a targeted 30% [3], while keeping emergency well-block shutdowns, Subsoil Code groundwater protection and financial statement verification under human control [1] [7].

The 5%–6% acid, 10-day TITR and 30% radiation figures are proposed pilot targets benchmarked against IAEA and Cameco documentation [3] [6]. They are not verified historical results from Kazatomprom.
